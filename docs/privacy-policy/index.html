Forgetty Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Forgetty is a task management application developed and maintained by Serhii Domin (si13n). This Privacy Policy explains what information Forgetty may process, why it is processed, how it is stored, and what choices you have.

1. Information we process

Guest mode

You can use Forgetty without signing in. In guest mode, task data and app preferences are stored locally on your device. This may include task titles, completion status, notes, due dates, reminders, lists, tags, subtasks, recurrence settings, and similar task-related information that you choose to enter.

Forgetty does not upload guest task data to the developer's servers unless you later sign in and explicitly choose to import local tasks into your signed-in account.

Signed-in mode

If you choose to sign in with Google, Forgetty uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. Authentication may provide the app with basic account information such as your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image.

When signed in, your task data is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so that it can be associated with your account and synchronized across app sessions and supported devices.

Voice input

Forgetty may allow you to create task text using the Android speech recognition service. Audio processing is handled by the speech recognition provider available on your device. Forgetty receives the recognized text and does not itself record or store the original audio.

Notifications and reminders

Forgetty may schedule local notifications for reminders, due-today alerts, and similar task notifications. Notification scheduling is performed on the device. Forgetty does not use notification content for advertising or profiling.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide and operate Forgetty features, including:

  • authenticating signed-in users;
  • storing, displaying, organizing, and synchronizing tasks;
  • importing guest tasks when a user chooses to do so;
  • scheduling reminders and task notifications;
  • displaying account information in the app; and
  • maintaining app functionality and security.

Forgetty does not sell personal data and does not use personal data for third-party advertising.

3. Third-party services

Forgetty uses services provided by Google, including:

  • Google Sign-In / Google Identity Services for sign-in;
  • Firebase Authentication for account authentication; and
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore for cloud storage of signed-in task data.

If you use voice input, your device's speech recognition provider may process audio according to that provider's own privacy policy.

These providers may process technical or account data as necessary to provide their services. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Data sharing

Forgetty does not sell or rent your personal information. Data is shared only with service providers when necessary to operate the app, such as Google/Firebase for authentication and cloud storage, or a device speech recognition provider when you intentionally use voice input.

5. Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data handled by Forgetty. Communication with Firebase services is transmitted using encrypted network connections provided by Google/Firebase. Local app data is stored within Android app storage and is subject to the security controls of your device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Data retention and deletion

Guest data

Guest task data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to standard Android backup and restore behavior configured on your device.

Signed-in data

Cloud task data is retained while your account is active or until the data is deleted. Individual tasks and completed tasks can be deleted from within Forgetty.

Account deletion

You may request deletion of your Forgetty account and associated cloud data at any time.

To request deletion, email si13n@yahoo.com with the subject “Forgetty account deletion request” and send the request from the email address associated with your Forgetty account, or clearly identify that account in your message.

After the request is verified, the Forgetty account data under the developer's control, including associated cloud task data, will be permanently deleted unless retention is required by law, security, fraud-prevention, or another legitimate legal obligation.

Deleting your Forgetty account does not delete your Google account.

7. Children's privacy

Forgetty is a general-purpose productivity application and is not specifically directed to children. The developer does not knowingly use the app to collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Forgetty features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.
